**Fig 1. Label-free scoring method and evaluation design.**
(A) Scoring pipeline. Gene-effect profiles (DepMap CRISPRGeneEffect, 1,361 cell lines × 18,434 genes) are standardised per gene. The cell-line covariance Σ is estimated from 17,525 genes with less than 10% missing values. Its top 20 principal components are removed, and the remainder is whitened with a ridge term, (λ + 0.1·λ̄)^−1/2. F is the correlation of the filtered profiles. Each gene's top 0.25% partners (about 44 genes), ranked by row-standardised and symmetrised F, define neighbour lists. B is the cosine of two genes' neighbour vectors, with the pair itself excluded, followed by a genome-wide row z-score. E is the cosine similarity in a 512-dimensional Laplacian eigenmap of the union neighbour graph. Each view is converted to a z-score using fixed moments from 200,000 random gene pairs, and zavg_BEF is the mean of the three z-scores. No step uses labels.
(B) Evaluation design. Clusters are connected components of the graph of hu.MAP 3.0 training positive pairs; the 144-protein component was split into 7 Louvain communities (247 clusters). About 25% of positives, chosen by cluster, formed the internal final test (IFT; 57 clusters, 847 positive and 40,786 negative pairs), which was evaluated once with candidates fixed in advance. The development set (190 clusters) was used for all method selection, with five-fold cross-validation grouped by cluster and repeated with five seeds. Pairs between a development protein and an IFT protein were excluded. The primary metric, HT-AP, weights each pair by the inverse of its probability of entering the test set, so that the evaluated pairs have the natural prevalence (0.53%).

**Fig 2. Performance on the development set.**
(A) HT-AP from five-fold cross-validation grouped by cluster. Points are the five seed repeats (each a mean over five test folds); bars and numbers give the five-seed mean. Baselines use their best variant selected on development data (signed scores). F, B and E are the components of zavg_BEF.
(B) Difference in HT-AP from GLS on pooled out-of-fold predictions, for each seed. Error bars are 95% intervals from a paired cluster bootstrap (1,000 resamples over 190 clusters).
(C) Left, macro AP: the unweighted mean over clusters of the HT-AP of pairs touching each cluster. Right, HT-AP on pairs that touch none of the 10 clusters with the most positives. B matches GLS on macro AP (0.351 vs 0.349), whereas zavg_BEF improves on it (0.395).

**Fig 3. Contribution of each step, from GLS-like whitening to zavg_BEF.**
HT-AP on the development set, split seed 0, mean over five folds. Each row adds one change to the row above, and the number in parentheses is the increment. The first row is GLS-like whitening (no components removed, no ridge); it differs from GLS (vertical line, 0.288) in imputation and in using a cosine instead of a t-statistic. These values come from the selection stage on the same split used to choose the configuration among about 300 variants, so they are optimistic. On this seed, E alone scores 0.460; zavg_BEF was chosen by its five-seed mean (0.470 vs 0.463 for E; Fig 2A).

**Fig 4. Where the gain over GLS comes from (development set, split seed 0).**
(A, B) Difference in per-cluster AP from GLS for zavg_BEF (A) and B (B), against the number of positive pairs touching the cluster (log scale). Each point is one of 177 clusters with both positive and negative pairs; open circles mark the 10 clusters with the most positives. The black line is a Gaussian-kernel weighted mean (bandwidth 0.3 decades). zavg_BEF exceeds GLS in 93 clusters and falls below it in 57 (mean difference +0.042); for B the counts are 73 and 80 (mean +0.001).
(C) HT-AP on pairs touching the 10 largest clusters (open circles; 1,456 positives) and on all other pairs (filled; 808 positives). Without the largest clusters, B (0.339) is close to F (0.343) and above GLS (0.299); zavg_BEF is highest (0.363).

**Fig 5. Single evaluation on the internal final test.**
IFT: 847 positive and 40,786 negative pairs in 57 clusters. The candidates were fixed in advance (zavg_BEF primary, B secondary); baselines are the variants selected on development data.
(A) HT-weighted precision–recall curves; the legend gives HT-AP. The grey line marks the weighted prevalence (0.0055).
(B) HT-AP by essentiality of the pair. A gene is common-essential if its gene effect is below −0.5 in at least half of the cell lines. Positive pairs: 286 with no essential gene, 195 mixed and 366 with two essential genes.
(C) Macro AP over 56 clusters with positives, and HT-AP excluding pairs that touch the largest connected component (578 positives remain). zavg_BEF is non-inferior to GLS on macro AP (0.365 vs 0.369); B is not (0.296), which is why B was not confirmed. Confidence intervals for the differences are given in the text.
