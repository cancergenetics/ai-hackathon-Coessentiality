# REPORT — Predicting co-complex membership from DepMap co-essentiality

Date: 2026-10-27. Record: `RESEARCH_TREE.md` (decisions D0–D4), `DESIGN.md` v3.1, all tables generated by `scripts/40_report_tables.py` → `results/report/tables.md` (T1–T7). Every number below comes from a committed result file.

## 1. Bottom line
- **Final method `zavg_BEF`** is a fully label-free co-essentiality score: an equal-weight average of three z-scored similarity views. All three are computed from gene-standardised, PC-filtered, whitened CRISPRGeneEffect profiles.
- **Dev results** (cluster-grouped, gene-disjoint 5-fold CV, 5 seed repeats; Horvitz–Thompson-weighted AUPRC, "HT-AP", at natural prevalence):

| method | dev HT-AP |
|---|---|
| **zavg_BEF** | **0.470** |
| GLS | 0.312 |
| Spearman | 0.282 |
| Pearson | 0.260 |

  - Pooled-OOF ΔAP vs GLS was +0.175 to +0.193, with the 95% cluster-bootstrap CI above 0 in 5/5 seeds.
- **Internal final test (IFT)** is a single pre-registered look on held-out complexes (847 positive / 40,786 negative pairs):

| method | IFT HT-AP | Δ vs zavg_BEF's baseline gain [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|
| **zavg_BEF** | **0.345** | – |
| GLS | 0.242 | +0.103 [+0.035, +0.209] (+42%) |
| Spearman | 0.146 | +0.199 [+0.099, +0.321] |
| Pearson | 0.134 | +0.211 [+0.103, +0.338] |

  - zavg_BEF meets the pre-registered "clearly beats" criterion against all three baselines (DESIGN §3.7).
  - An independent re-implementation from raw files reproduced it exactly (`results/verify/`).
- **Withheld hu.MAP test set:** not accessed. Score it with the frozen method using `scripts/predict_pairs.py` (§7).
- **Runtime:** 137 s from the raw CSV to scores for 635k pairs on an 8-core laptop. GLS takes 24 s, Pearson under 1 s.

## 2. Data and evaluation (DESIGN v3.1)
**Data**
- DepMap 26Q3 CRISPRGeneEffect: 1,361 models × 18,434 genes.
- hu.MAP train pairs: 3,314 positive / 631,465 negative over 1,151 UniProt IDs; 1,114 IDs map to DepMap genes via HGNC → Entrez.
- Unmappable genes are 37 IDs: immunoglobulin segments, mtDNA-encoded, haemoglobin and multi-copy histone genes.
- Sources, checksums and licences are in DATA.md.

**Why random pair splits are inadequate**
- Negatives are essentially all non-positive pairs among the same proteins.
- hu.MAP's test split is complex-level.
- So the primary evaluation keeps proteins disjoint between train and test.

**How the splits were built**
- **Clusters.** Connected components of the positive graph form 247 clusters. The 144-protein giant component, which holds 26% of positives, is split into Louvain communities.
- **IFT.** About 25% of positives were held out by stratified cluster assignment and looked at once.
- **Dev CV.** 5-fold cluster-grouped CV on the rest; cross pairs are unused.

**Primary metric**
- HT-weighted AP: between-cluster pairs are up-weighted by their inverse inclusion probability, so fold composition matches the natural 0.53% prevalence and the natural share of hard negatives.
- Secondary metrics: macro per-cluster AP, AP without the largest components, AP by essentiality stratum, and AUROC.

**Uncertainty and gates**
- CIs come from a paired cluster bootstrap.
- A power simulation showed the IFT (Kish n_eff ≈ 7.7 clusters) cannot reliably support a CI-based gate (D2). Before any look, the gate became:
  - a dev pooled-OOF CI excluding 0, and
  - an IFT point estimate of at least +10% plus non-inferiority (≥ 95% of the baseline) on macro and no-largest-component AP.
- Candidates were tested in fixed sequence.

**Baselines**
- Pearson, Spearman (pairwise-complete) and GLS (Wainberg et al. 2021, exact matrix-form port, tested against a line-by-line port).
- Each baseline got its best dev-selected variant: signed > absolute; GLS without olfactory-receptor PC removal, which hurt.
- Covariance rank deficiency (1357/1361) is handled by dropping 4 dependent samples.

## 3. The method
All steps are label-free; the only inputs are CRISPRGeneEffect and the fixed configuration. Code: `src/layers.py`, `src/layers_ext.py`, `src/layers_graph.py`, `src/predict.py`.

**Base profiles**
1. Standardise each gene across cell lines. Genes with ≥10% NaN are mean-imputed and scored, but excluded from the covariance and neighbour sets.
2. Estimate the 1,361 × 1,361 cell-line covariance genome-wide (17.5k low-NaN genes).
3. Remove the top 20 eigencomponents and whiten the rest with (λ + 0.1·λ̄)^(-1/2). This is a ridge-regularised, PC-filtered GLS.

**Three views**
- **F (first order):** Pearson correlation of the filtered profiles.
- **B (second order):** keep each gene's top 0.25% genome-wide neighbours under F (about 44 genes). Take the cosine of the sparsified neighbour vectors, excluding the pair itself, then a genome-wide row-z (pair score = mean of the two row z-scores).
- **E (graph embedding):** build a union-kNN graph (q = 0.25%) on F, take a 512-dimensional Laplacian eigenmap, and use cosine similarity.

**Final score:** zavg_BEF = mean of the z-scores of B, E and F. The z-moments are fixed from 200k random genome-wide gene pairs, not from labelled pairs.

**Why it works** (dev evidence, T3)
- Standardising genes before estimating Σ: +0.06 over GLS-style whitening.
- Second-order neighbourhoods: +0.05–0.08. Complex subunits share co-dependency partners, which is more robust than their direct correlation.
- Weakness of B alone: the second-order gain is concentrated in large, dense complexes. Excluding the 10 largest clusters, B ≈ GLS, and macro per-cluster AP is no better.
- Adding F (direct similarity) and E (smooth graph geometry) fixes this. On dev, AP without the top-10 clusters rises by +0.024 to +0.031 over B (CI > 0 in 5/5 seeds) and macro AP goes from 0.351 to 0.395. On IFT, macro AP is 0.365 for zavg_BEF vs 0.296 for B and 0.369 for GLS.

## 5. What did not help (T6)
- **Supervised stacking** (LR/LightGBM over 7 views, with or without gene-level essentiality features) was never better than the equal-weight z-average: 5-seed 0.462 / 0.466 vs 0.470.
- **Gene features** carry almost no transferable signal on gene-disjoint folds.
- **Supervised metric learning** (cell-line weights, low-rank InfoNCE/BCE projections, second order on the learned space) never beat its initialisation. Models memorise the ~650 training genes, and validation on unseen complexes stays flat. With about 3k positives in about 190 clusters, labels add nothing beyond the label-free geometry.
- **Other variants:**
  - Olfactory-receptor PC removal for GLS: hurt.
  - Data subsets (R1 data review, label-free probes on all pairs, not dev-CV): restricting CRISPRGeneEffect to Avana-screened models gave no gain after PC removal; ScreenGeneEffect Avana-only screens were worse (0.149 vs 0.223). See manuscript/S1_Text.md §2.
  - TOM, soft-thresholding, multi-step diffusion: no gain.
  - Greedy ensembles: their dev gain vanished under nested selection.

## 6. Limitations and caveats
- **Gain concentration.** Gains are largest for big, dense complexes. The IFT macro per-cluster AP of the final method only equals GLS (0.365 vs 0.369). On small complexes, our advantage over GLS is modest.
- **IFT size and look history.**
  - The IFT is small in effective size (n_eff ≈ 8 clusters), so its CIs are wide; the dev evidence carries the statistical weight.
  - Before the IFT existed, round-1 design reviewers computed label-free AP probes on all pairs (D1). No selection was made from those probes, but the whitening-family prior was IFT-informed.
- **Dev optimism.** The configuration (k = 20, τ = 0.1, q = 0.25%, d = 512) was selected on dev from about 300 variants. Nested checks put optimism at 0.002–0.01. The +0.02 margin of zavg_BEF over B is stable in size across E settings, but whether it is significant depends on the configuration (verifier).
- **Proximity artefact.** In the olfactory-receptor control, 7.2% of OR×OR pairs score above the null 99th percentile (GLS 6.6%). Same-chromosome OR clusters drive this, which suggests the second-order view slightly amplifies a CRISPR proximity or cross-cutting artefact.
- **Ceiling from unmapped proteins.** About 3.7% of train positives involve proteins without a DepMap profile (Ig segments, mtDNA genes, multi-copy histones/haemoglobins); they get the minimum score. Expect a similar ceiling on the withheld test.
- **Release dependence.** The DepMap release is user-stated (26Q3); the files carry no version stamp. The covariance is rank-deficient in this release (4 dependent samples).
- **Composition dependence.** Results depend on hu.MAP 3.0's complex composition (≤ 30 subunits). Very large complexes are not represented.

## 7. Scoring the withheld test set
```bash
./.env/bin/python scripts/predict_pairs.py --pairs Test_pairs.txt --method zavg:B,E,F --out scores.tsv
```
- **Input:** two tab-separated UniProt columns, no header.
- **Output:** `uniprot_a, uniprot_b, score, mapped`. Unmapped pairs get the file minimum − 1.
- **Run record:** `scores.tsv.run.json` holds the git commit, input SHA-256s and runtimes.
- **Baselines:** `--method gls|pearson|spearman`. Secondary candidate: `--method single`.
- **Reproducibility:** scores are batch- and order-invariant and deterministic. The environment is in `requirements.txt` (native arm64 Python 3.12 env `.env/`).

## 8. Follow-ups and remaining ideas
1. **Small complexes.** Down-weight neighbours that are hub or common-essential genes in the second-order step. Alternatively, apply per-complex-size adaptive q (local scaling as in self-tuning spectral clustering).
2. **Artefact correction.** Regress CRISPR proximity effects (same-arm or cytoband neighbours, OR clusters) out of the profiles before building the kNN graph.
3. **Paralog and multi-copy proteins.** Score proteins encoded by several gene copies with the mean or max over copies, to recover the dropped histones.
4. **Faster E.** Use a randomised or Lanczos eigensolver on the sparse Laplacian to cut runtime about 3×. This is an engineering change, not a statistical one.
5. **Other DepMap inputs** (would need a request to the user): RNAi (DEMETER2) profiles or guide-level data as an independent view for the z-average. Replication across perturbation modalities could discount CRISPR-specific artefacts.
6. **Robust consensus over DepMap releases or bootstrap resamples of cell lines.** Average B/E/F over cell-line subsamples to stabilise the kNN graph; the eigenmap shows near-degenerate spectra at d = 512 (verifier).
