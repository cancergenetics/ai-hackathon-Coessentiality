# DESIGN.md — co-complex prediction from DepMap co-essentiality

Version: v3 (2026-10-27, after review rounds 1–2; reviews in `reviews/round1/ and reviews/round2/`). Frozen for method development. Revision log at bottom.
Fixed by PROMPT.md: task, data sources, AUPRC objective, baselines (Pearson, Spearman, GLS), leakage-aware evaluation, withheld test.

## 1. Problem
Input: unordered UniProt pair. Output: score (higher = co-complex). Allowed inputs: DepMap CRISPR files + metadata, HGNC, hu.MAP *train* labels. Target: AUPRC on the withheld hu.MAP test pairs, built (Fischer et al. 2025) from a random 50:50 split of Complex Portal complexes (≤30 subunits, Jaccard-deduplicated); negatives = protein pairs in separate complexes. Test pairs therefore mostly involve unseen complexes; proteins may overlap if shared between complexes. Expected test prevalence ≈ train prevalence (≈0.5%).

## 2. Data facts that drive the design
- 3,314 pos / 631,465 neg over 1,151 UniProt IDs (prevalence 0.52%). 27,046 pairs among these proteins are in neither file (**unlabelled; never used, never treated as negatives; unit-tested**), since they may relate to test complexes.
- Negatives ≈ all non-positive labelled pairs → protein-level propensity leaks under random pair splits.
- Positive graph: 232 components; the largest (144 proteins) holds ≈26% of positives; top 3 ≈43%. Large complexes (>30) absent, so ribosome/complex-I dominance is not the issue; one giant component is.
- Labelled genes are 31% common-essential vs 9% genome-wide; ~40% of positives have both genes non-essential and are much harder (AP ≈0.08 vs ≈0.3).
- Mapping UniProt → HGNC (Approved) → Entrez → DepMap column; ~1,116 mapped IDs; ambiguity rules logged in `src/data.py`.

## 3. Evaluation protocol (frozen before method development)
3.1 **Clusters.** Approximate complexes = communities of the train-positive graph: connected components; components with >40 proteins are split by seeded Louvain/greedy-modularity (networkx) into communities. Positive edges that cross communities are reported. Cluster = unit of splitting and of bootstrap.
3.2 **Internal final test (IFT).** Seed `SEEDS[0]`: cluster assignment to IFT until ≈25% of positives, stratified by cluster size (top-10 clusters spread; draws with IFT Kish n_eff < 0.2× total rejected). `scripts/power_ift.py` reports probability of passing §3.7 at true relative gains of 10/15/20%. IFT pairs = both proteins in IFT clusters. Cross pairs (dev × IFT protein) excluded from training and evaluation. Proteins in both (via cross-community positive edges) are assigned to one side only; their cross pairs are dropped.
3.3 **Development CV (selection).** Remaining clusters, 5-fold cluster-grouped CV with randomised (not largest-first) balanced assignment; train = pairs among training-fold proteins; test = pairs among held-out fold proteins; cross-fold pairs unused. Exploration uses `SEEDS[0]`; final comparisons repeat over all 5 seeds.
3.4 **Secondary splits (report only).** (a) strict connected-component grouping (no community splitting); (b) random protein-disjoint C3; (c) random pair-level 5-fold (leaky contrast). C1/C2/C3 breakdown for supervised models.
3.5 **Metrics.** Primary: **Horvitz–Thompson-weighted AP** per fold, mean over folds: within-cluster pairs weight 1, between-cluster pairs weight K (=5 folds; on IFT 1/f with f the realised IFT cluster fraction), so the evaluated pair mix matches the full universe (natural prevalence, correct share of hard negatives). Uniform-reweighted AP is secondary. Label-free scorers additionally: AP pooled over all dev pairs (natural prevalence). Secondary: raw AP, AUROC, precision at recall 0.1/0.25/0.5, macro per-cluster AP, AP without the largest component, AP restricted to hard negatives (same component), AP stratified by pair essentiality (both non-essential / mixed / both essential). Runtime (wall clock) for every method.
3.6 **Uncertainty.** Paired cluster bootstrap (1,000 resamples over clusters; within-cluster pairs weight m_c, between-cluster pairs m_c1·m_c2), seeded.
3.7 **"Clearly beats" (pre-registered).** Against each baseline in its best dev-selected variant (intersection–union: must hold vs all three): (i) higher HT-AP in ≥4/5 dev folds for SEEDS[0] and higher mean in each of 5 seed repeats (split-variance check, not fresh evidence); (ii) dev: 95% paired cluster-bootstrap CI of ΔHT-AP on pooled out-of-fold dev predictions (190 clusters) excludes 0 in each seed repeat, AND IFT: point estimate ≥10% relative HT-AP gain (IFT CI reported, not required — v3.1 amendment, see log); (iii) non-inferiority (≥ −5% relative) on macro per-cluster AP and AP-without-largest-component. Multiplicity: ≤2 candidates declared before the IFT look, tested in fixed sequence (primary first). The primary is submitted as the final method whatever the IFT shows; the final model is refit on all train pairs (dev+IFT) with frozen hyper-parameters.
3.8 **Baselines.** Pearson, Spearman (pairwise-complete), GLS faithful to Wainberg 2021 (OR-gene 4-PC removal, Cholesky of pinv(cov), per-gene lstsq with warped intercept, t-stat) with a documented NaN protocol (variants: mean-impute; drop NaN genes from Σ estimation; literal cell-line drop) — best variant (signed vs |·|, OR removal on/off, NaN protocol) chosen on dev, same tuning budget as ours. Every method scores the same pair universe (all mapped genes; imputation where needed). Extra strong reference: PC-removed Pearson ("onion" layer) is *our* method family, not a baseline.
3.9 **Controls.** Positive: toy with planted complexes + planted cell-line covariance (GLS > Pearson; method recovers planted pairs). Negative: label-permuted training (held fold and all labels) → AP ≈ prevalence; degree-preserving label permutation; gene-stat-only model (own essentiality stats, no pair similarity) — full model must beat it with CI excluding 0, and gene features are judged against a similarity-only model; OR-gene pairs genome-wide score as background; chromosome-proximity control.
3.10 **Unmapped pairs.** Excluded from dev metrics (counted). Final predictor gives them the minimum score; IFT also reported with them included under that rule.

## 4. Method plan
Phase A — label-free similarity layers, implemented as label-free functions f(X, ia, ib) cached genome-wide before any split; one screening sweep on dev, then configs frozen before Phase B:
 A1 Spectral-filter whitening family on CRISPRGeneEffect: Σ (lines×lines) estimated genome-wide (genes <10% NaN, mean-imputed), never on labelled genes only; filters = top-k PC removal (k sweep) × ridge/shrinkage τ (Ledoit–Wolf etc.); GLS is a special case.
 A2 Optional confounder regression (CRISPRConfounders QC, library flags, CasActivity, doubling time + missingness indicators) before PCs.
 A3 Rank/row balancing with genome-wide background: row-z of similarity rows, mutual rank (raw and balanced versions kept).
 A4 Second-order similarity: sparsified (top 1%) neighbour-overlap / correlation of similarity rows.
 A5 Avana-only, ScreenGeneEffect per-library, lineage-specific: exploratory only (R1 data review: SGE Avana-only worse).
 A6 Label-free ensemble: z-averaging of best layers with z-moments from a frozen genome-wide null (not from evaluated pairs/folds) — required candidate and default primary unless a supervised model wins on dev.
Phase B — supervised pair model: shallow LightGBM (monotone in similarity features), and logistic regression; ~10–20 Phase-A layers + symmetric gene-reliability features (min/max of mean effect, SD, fraction dependent, NaN fraction) + chromosome features as diagnostic/ablation only. Gene features coarsened (≤5 quantile bins), num_leaves ≤7, min_child_samples ≥2,000 so trees cannot isolate single proteins; gene-stat-only identifiability check. Bagged negative subsampling, no class weights, cluster-balanced sample weights, one `fit(train_idx)` interface; inner cluster-grouped validation. Ablation: LightGBM over Pearson+Spearman+GLS only (does supervision alone explain gains?).
Phase C (low priority, time-boxed ~3 h) — learned metric (capacity ladder, stop when no gain; adopted only if it also wins under strict-component grouping): learned spectral filter → learned cell-line weights shrunk to GLS → low-rank linear projection trained with InfoNCE/BCE on train-fold pairs. Scores depend only on the two profiles (applies to unseen genes).
Phase D — final ensemble of best label-free and supervised scores (z-averaging or stacking within CV).
Deprioritised: A2 confounder regression and A5 (off critical path), graphical lasso, SNF, deep Siamese networks, dictionary learning, Spearman/bicor after whitening.

## 5. Reproducibility
Seeds `[0,1,2,3,4]`. Scripts → JSON in `results/` with `run_record()` (git commit, dirty flag, input/cache hashes, seed, pair counts, runtime). All variants logged in `results/trials.jsonl`. Caches verified by hash on load; `scripts/check_data.sh` at script start. Split files regenerated from seed; SHA-256 committed. `scripts/predict_pairs.py` scores any pair file with the frozen final model. REPORT tables generated by script.

## 6. Agent-process safeguards
- IFT pairs accessible only via `scripts/final_eval.py`, which appends to `results/ift_looks.jsonl`. No metrics on labels outside dev folds.
- Tests: batch/order invariance of scores; end-to-end ID chain (30 pairs recomputed from raw CSV); no gene-ID features reach models; unlabelled pairs never loaded as negatives; splits disjointness.
- Independent verification: a separate agent reruns headline results from a clean checkout with caches deleted and re-implements AP and disjointness checks without importing `src/metrics.py`.
- Handbacks use a fixed format: what was run (script+commit), numbers, checks passed, deviations.
- Only `scripts/final_eval.py` imports the IFT loader (AST test). `ift_looks.jsonl` starts with look #0 = R1 all-pairs probe (D1). `log_trial()` writes start and end records per grid point. Results are refused on a dirty `src/` unless `--allow-dirty` (recorded).
- A failed control is a finding and is recorded, not re-run until it passes. Nothing enters RESEARCH_TREE as [x]/[!] without tests passing and toy controls.

## 7. Compute
8 cores, 24 GB, MPS. Work with labelled genes × all genes (1.1k × 18k) similarity; whitening via eigendecomposition of 1,361×1,361 Σ (cheap).
