Bins per statistic (genome-wide quantile edges over all 18,434 genes): {'mean': 5, 'sd': 5, 'skew': 5, 'frac_dep': 5, 'nan_frac': 2, 'min': 5, 'p05': 5}. ## Findings (seed 0, provisional layer set; written after the run)

1. (a) Supervision alone over Pearson + Spearman + GLS: LGBM +0.026 fold-mean HT-AP over GLS (pooled OOF +0.008, CI includes 0); LR +0.034 (pooled +0.040, CI [+0.012, +0.075]). A small, real gain from re-weighting the three baselines; much smaller than the gain from new layers.
2. (d) LGBM over the 12 provisional layers: 0.383 vs GLS 0.288 (pooled ΔOOF +0.066, CI [+0.001, +0.153]); but the label-free unweighted z-mean of the same 12 layers already reaches 0.357 (Δ LGBM − z-mean pooled +0.006, CI [−0.034, +0.032]). Most of the gain is the layers, not the supervision.
3. Gene features: binned gene features add +0.002 (fold mean) / +0.033 (pooled, CI includes 0) to LGBM and nothing to LR; raw gene features +0.018 / +0.037 (CI includes 0) and the largest excess leakage gap. Chromosome features: +0.001 / +0.023 (CI includes 0); chromosome-only model = prevalence.
4. Controls: gene-stat-only models give HT-AP 0.019–0.024 vs HT prevalence 0.009 (≈2.5× prevalence, transferable essentiality propensity, 6% of GLS); identical under cluster, C3 and random splits (no memorisation inflation: the leaf constraints work). Held-fold shuffle = prevalence. All-label shuffle is NOT ≈ prevalence for monotone LGBM (0.146; every shuffled model correlates ρ ≈ 0.78 with GLS) because the monotone constraint itself encodes 'more similar = more likely'; without constraints the median is at prevalence (0.009), and for LR the shuffled fit picks a random direction in the 3-feature similarity space (AP 0.121 when ρ > 0, 0.014 when ρ ≤ 0). Not leakage: permuted labels carry no information; gene-only shuffle = prevalence.


## Model details

- LightGBM: num_leaves 7, min_child_samples 2,000, learning rate 0.05, row/column subsample 0.8, L2 1, monotone +1 on every similarity feature (layers, baselines), unconstrained gene/chromosome features. 5 bags; each = all training positives + 50 × n_pos random training negatives; per bag early stopping (100 rounds, AP) on an inner cluster-grouped validation split of the training fold (inner fold 1/4 of training clusters, val pairs un-subsampled), then refit on the whole bag with the best iteration count. Score = mean raw margin over bags.
- Logistic regression: StandardScaler + L2 (C = 1) per bag, same bags and weights, mean decision function.
- Sample weights: positives 1/sqrt(n_pos touching their cluster) (mean over the two clusters if the positive spans two), rescaled to mean 1; negatives 1. No class weights.
- Layer features are z-scored with the manifest genome-wide null moments (affects LR only; trees are invariant). Baseline features are the raw scores of `src/scorers` (Pearson/Spearman pairwise-complete, GLS marginalised t).
- Gene features (`src/gene_features.py`): mean, sd, skew, fraction of lines < −0.5, NaN fraction, min, 5th percentile per gene; ≤ 5 genome-wide quantile bins; pair features min/max per statistic (`generaw` = raw values, ablation). Chromosome: same chromosome, same arm (HGNC cytoband).
- Inner validation always uses the cluster-grouped scheme, also when the outer split is gene/random.
- Per-run JSON: fold metrics, run_record, `phaseB.pooled_oof`, feature importances (LGBM gain share / LR standardised coefficients), per-bag early-stopping logs. OOF score arrays: data_cache/phaseB_oof/ (gitignored).
